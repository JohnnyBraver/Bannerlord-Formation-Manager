[size=4][b]Description[/b][/size]
[size=3][color=#D4D4D8]Formation Manager lets you set persistent troop and role-based formation defaults, then fine-tune each battle in Bannerlord's Order of Battle screen. Your defaults organize the first layout without taking control away from you.[/color][/size]

[size=4][b]Installation instructions[/b][/size]
[size=3][color=#D4D4D8]1. Extract the downloaded archive into your Mount & Blade II Bannerlord game directory (specifically inside the Modules folder).
2. Ensure the "FormationManager" folder is present under Modules.
3. Enable "Stop Shuffling, You Fools - Formation Manager" in the game launcher.[/color][/size]

[size=4][b]Main features[/b][/size]
[size=3][color=#D4D4D8]- Party Screen Badges: Assign troops to formation numbers (I-VIII) directly from the party screen.
- Two-Formation Splits: Give a troop stack a second formation badge to split it evenly between two groups.
- Troop Role Defaults: Configure slots for light infantry, shield infantry, shock, pike, skirmisher, archer, crossbow, cavalry, and horse-archer roles. Role classification is supplied by the required Troop Classifier module.
- Editable Order of Battle: Start from your defaults, then adjust formation types and native OOB weights for the current battle.
- Persistent Assignments: Assignments are saved automatically per campaign main hero.[/color][/size]

[size=4][b]Requirements[/b][/size]
[size=3][color=#D4D4D8]- Harmony
- UIExtenderEx
- MCM (Mod Configuration Menu)
- Troop Classifier[/color][/size]
